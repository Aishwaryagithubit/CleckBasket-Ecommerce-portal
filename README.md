# CleckBasket-Ecommerce-portal
CleckBasket is an e-commerce platform designed for independent local food retailers.It allows customers to browse products from multiple shops, place orders in a shared basket, and schedule click and collect pickups, helping small businesses compete with large retail chains while maintaining their local identity.

![image alt](https://github.com/Aishwaryagithubit/CleckBasket-Ecommerce-portal/blob/2d30dffa75f22b5c8ed287fa9a0832d6147f3686/CleckBasket_HZ.png)

**Key Features**

🛍 Multi Vendor Marketplace
Customers can shop from multiple local traders in a single platform.

🧺 Shared Shopping Basket
Add products from different shops and complete a single checkout.

⏱ Click-and-Collect Ordering
Customers select a convenient collection slot for their orders.

🔐 User Account System
Secure user registration and login for order management.

💳 Integrated Online Payments
Supports secure payment processing (e.g. PayPal).

📦 Trader Product Management
Traders can add, update, and manage their product listings.

📊 Management Dashboard
Provides reporting on orders, stock levels, and financial performance.

📱 Responsive Web Design
Accessible across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

**Project Goals**

The primary goal of CleckBasket is to empower independent local businesses by providing them with digital tools typically available only to large retailers.

The platform aims to:

Improve accessibility to local produce for busy customers

Help small traders remain competitive with large retail chains

Enable businesses to extend their market reach without extending physical store hours

Promote sustainable local commerce and community-driven retail

The System Limitations (Pilot Scope)

To ensure manageable development and testing, the prototype includes several operational constraints:

Initial participation limited to 5 traders

Maximum system capacity of 10 shops

Click-and-collect only (no delivery service)

Fixed collection time slots

Orders must be placed 24 hours in advance

These limitations allow the system to be evaluated on a smaller scale before considering broader deployment.

**Future Improvements**

Potential future enhancements include :

Supporting additional traders and product categories

Introducing home delivery services

Expanding collection slots and scheduling flexibility

Advanced inventory management and analytics

Enhanced UI/UX and mobile-first design

Integration with additional payment gateways
