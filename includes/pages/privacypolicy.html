Privacy Policy
Effective date: 1 January 2026  ·  Last updated: 1 January 2026
Overview
CleckBasket ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the personal information of all users of our platform. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, the purposes for which it is used, how it is stored, and the rights you have regarding your information. By registering for or using CleckBasket, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
Information we collect
Account data — your name, email address, phone number, and a hashed version of your password, provided when you register.
Order data — items purchased, collection slot selections, order history, and payment confirmation records.
Payment data — transaction amounts and payment method type. Full card details are handled exclusively by our PCI-compliant payment processor and are never stored by CleckBasket.
Usage data — pages visited, features used, and browser or device type, collected automatically to help us improve the platform.
How we use your data

We use your personal information solely to provide and improve the CleckBasket service — including processing orders, sending transactional emails (order confirmations, collection reminders), and maintaining account security. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Processing and fulfilling your collection orders with local traders.
Sending transactional communications such as order confirmations and OTP verification codes.
Improving platform performance and resolving technical issues.
Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
Data security
We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing and are never held in plain text. Access to personal data within our systems is restricted to authorised personnel only.
Your rights
Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to:
Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Rectification — ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
Erasure — request deletion of your account and associated personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Withdrawal of consent — opt out of any optional data processing at any time.
To exercise any of your rights or for questions about this policy, contact our team at privacy@cleckbasket.com. We aim to respond to all requests within 30 days.
