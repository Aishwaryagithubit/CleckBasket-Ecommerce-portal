Overview
At CleckBasket, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. By using our services, you agree to the practices described here.
Information we collect
Account data — name, email address, password hash, and profile preferences you provide on registration.
Usage data — pages visited, features used, timestamps, and browser/device type collected automatically.
Payment data — billing address and last four digits of card; full card numbers are handled by our PCI-compliant processor and never stored by us.
Cookies — session tokens and analytics cookies to keep you signed in and improve performance.
How we use your data

We use your information to provide and improve our services, send transactional emails (receipts, security alerts), personalize your experience, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
