Collection Service
Pick Up Information
Wednesday, Thursday & Friday  ·  Orders placed at least 24 hours in advance
How it works
CleckBasket operates a collection-only service connecting you with local traders. Customers must register before placing orders and select a collection time slot on Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday. All orders must be placed a minimum of 24 hours before the chosen slot, giving traders the time they need to prepare your fresh, local produce.

This system provides greater convenience for busy customers by allowing you to place orders outside normal shop opening hours, while still supporting the local businesses and traders at the heart of your community.

How to place a collection order
1

Register or log in — A registered CleckBasket account is required before placing any order.

2

Browse & add to cart — Shop from local traders across bakery, butcher, greengrocer, fishmonger, and delicatessen categories.

3

Choose your slot — Select a day (Wed / Thu / Fri) and a time window (10–13, 13–16, or 16–19), at least 24 hours ahead.

4

Confirm & pay — Traders are notified and will have your order ready for collection at your chosen time.

Important notes
Registration required — Only registered customers can browse trader stock and place collection orders.
24-hour minimum — Slots within the next 24 hours are automatically disabled.
Slot capacity — Each slot is limited to 20 orders. Fully booked slots are marked unavailable — book early.
Collection only — CleckBasket does not offer home delivery. All orders must be collected in person.
Questions about collection slots? Contact us at support@cleckbasket.com — we're happy to help you shop local.
