How it works
CleckBasket operates a collection-only service, connecting you directly with your favourite local traders. Customers must register before placing orders and select a collection time slot on Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday. All orders must be placed a minimum of 24 hours before the chosen slot, giving traders the time they need to prepare your fresh, local produce.

This system provides greater convenience for busy customers by allowing you to place orders outside normal shop opening hours, while still supporting the local businesses and traders at the heart of your community.

How to place a collection order
1

Register or log in — You must have a registered CleckBasket account before you can place an order. Sign up for free in minutes.

2

Browse & add to cart — Shop from your favourite local traders across our bakery, butcher, greengrocer, fishmonger, and delicatessen categories.

3

Choose your collection slot — Select a day (Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday) and your preferred time window: 10–13, 13–16, or 16–19. Slots must be booked at least 24 hours in advance.

4

Confirm & pay — Complete your order. Traders are notified immediately and will have your order ready for collection at your chosen time.

Important notes
Registration required — Only registered customers can browse trader stock and place collection orders.
24-hour minimum — Slots within the next 24 hours are automatically disabled. This ensures traders have adequate preparation time.
Slot availability — Each time slot is limited to 20 orders. Once a slot is full it will be marked unavailable. Book early to secure your preferred time.
Collection only — CleckBasket does not offer home delivery. All orders must be collected in person during your chosen time window.
